# Lunchtime — Performance, Progression & Exploration Overhaul

Lunchtime is getting a major overhaul focused on performance, cozy building, deeper exploration, better crafting progression, and more reasons to play together.

## ⚡ Performance & Stability Overhaul

• Added performance-focused utilities, including Concurrent Chunk Management Engine, Async Logger, AsyncParticles, Fast Noise Mod, FastALI, Fast Suite, Fast Item Frames, Fastvancements, and More Culling.

• Updated ModernFix and several supporting libraries and utilities.

• Added SparseStructures and NoStructureOverlap to help manage structure density and prevent unwanted overlaps.

• Continued performance testing and configuration cleanup, with particular attention to world generation and server stability.

• Added additional optimizations for item frames, furnaces, workbenches, advancement handling, and other client-side systems.

## 📖 Quests & Progression

• Added FTB Quests and FTB Quests UI Overhaul.

• Added a custom welcome screen and quest background artwork.

• Expanded the tools available for quest-based progression, multiplayer management, and rewards.

• Added Plushie Buddies, with plushies intended to become collectible quest rewards instead of ordinary craftable items.

• Added an extremely rare Star Wars reward to the reward-table setup.

• Expanded EMI and its supporting addons to provide a more integrated recipe-viewing experience, replacing the previous JEI-based setup.

• Continued work on making quest objectives, crafting requirements, and progression easier to understand.

## ⚒️ Crafting & Recipe Balancing

• Rebalanced Easy Villagers and worked on fixing the easy-villager farm.

• Fixed the Waystone crafting recipe.

• Rebalanced aircraft recipes.

• Added more recipe-viewing integrations for Create and other supported mods.

• Added Create EMI Compat, Create: EMI Schematics, and additional EMI integrations.

• Continued reviewing recipe balance and progression to keep farming, transportation, and automation useful without making everything too easy.

• Added Item Obliterator, allowing selected items and their associated recipes to be disabled through configuration.

## 💼 Jobs & Player Economy

• Added Jobs+ and Jobs+ Tools.

• Added Shoppy, expanding the possibilities for player shops and an in-game economy.

• Jobs+ includes professions such as Alchemist, Builder, Digger, Enchanter, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, Lumberjack, Miner, and Smith.

• These systems give players more ways to specialize, gather resources, earn rewards, and trade with other players.

• Custom professions, payouts, and additional roles such as bard or bartender may require further configuration or development.

## 🌍 World Generation & Exploration

• Added Integrated Dungeons and Structures, Integrated Villages, Moog's Mineshafts Reimagined, Moog's Temples Reimagined, and Moog's End Structures.

• Added Luki's Woodland Mansions and additional structure-generation content.

• Added Regions Unexplored for more world-generation variety.

• Added SparseStructures and NoStructureOverlap to help control structure placement and reduce unwanted overlaps.

• Reworked parts of the previous structure lineup, removing several older YUNG's structure modules while retaining selected ones.

• Continued work on structure spacing and disabling selected vanilla structures.

• Added additional dungeons, encounters, and exploration content to make discovering new areas more rewarding.

Explore new terrain, caves, structures, and encounters while keeping world generation focused on exploration rather than excessive structure density.

## 🍂 Seasons, Rivers & Underground Exploration

• Added Ecliptic Seasons, including integrations for Let's Do and Regions Unexplored.

• Added StreamsReflowing alongside RiverRedux for a refreshed river and outdoor-world experience.

• Added Spelunkery for additional cave and mining content.

• Added Icy's Better Horses for an improved horse experience.

• Added BetterDays to complement the seasonal direction.

• Continued refining environmental content to make exploration and the changing world feel more immersive.

## 🏡 Cozy Building & Decoration

• Removed Let's Do Furniture from the supplied mod list.

• Added Another Furniture, Cozy Home, Cozy Cafe, Dusty Decorations, and Building But Better.

• Retained established building and decoration options such as Chipped, Decorative Blocks, Supplementaries, and Immersive Paintings.

• Added Plushie Buddies for collectible decorations and quest rewards.

• Reworked the Quark configuration to disable selected decorative blocks, palettes, and building features that overlap with other content or do not fit the intended experience.

• Continued refining the building selection to keep Lunchtime cozy, accessible, and flexible for creative players.

## 🍳 Cooking, Farming & Food

• Added Barbeque's Delight, Brewin' And Chewin', Butchery, Fishermens Trap, Simple Ice Cream, and Kaleidoscope Grilling.

• Removed Ender's Delight, Let's Do Brewery, Let's Do Camping, and Let's Do HerbalBrews from the supplied new mod list.

• Retained the core Farmer's Delight ecosystem, including Cultural Delights, Delightful, Alex's Delight, Chef's Delight, and the existing Kaleidoscope cooking mods.

• Continued balancing Easy Villagers and related farm progression.

• Continued reviewing food and farming recipes to improve consistency and avoid unnecessary overlap.

The goal is to keep food, farming, and cooking central to the pack while making recipes and progression more consistent.

## 🐾 Mobs, Encounters & Additional Content

• Added Critters and Companions, Creeper Overhaul, Enderman Overhaul, Faunify, and Golem Overhaul.

• Added Cataclysm, Bosses'Rise, Combat Roll, DragonLoot, It Takes A Pillage, and other encounter-related content.

• Added additional creature, boss, and exploration features.

• Continued balancing mob spawns and difficulty to keep the world lively without overwhelming players or servers.

## 🎨 Interface, Audio & Multiplayer

• Added ImmersiveUI, Inventory Particles, Chat Heads, ChatAnimation, ExtraSounds Next, Minecraft Cursor, Emotecraft, and additional emote support.

• Added Chat Heads Figura Support for compatibility between Chat Heads and Figura.

• Added Exposure Polaroid and EMF Compat: Core.

• Added e4all and additional FTB multiplayer tools.

• Expanded chat functionality and continued reviewing chat-related configuration.

• Continued cleaning up keybinds and interface elements to reduce conflicts and improve usability.

• Reviewed Quark's optional client features, disabling selected controls and conveniences that conflict with the intended gameplay experience.

• Continued work on the Escape menu, default settings, resource-pack organization, and audio balancing.

• Kept Simple Voice Chat, Waystones, Xaero's Minimap, and Xaero's World Map in the listed setup.

## ⚙️ Quark Configuration Overhaul

• Reworked Quark's configuration to disable selected optional features across automation, building, management, tools, gameplay tweaks, world generation, mobs, and client settings.

• Disabled selected automation features, including Chute, Gravisand, Iron Rod, and Redstone Randomizer.

• Disabled selected building features and decorative variants to reduce overlap with other building mods.

• Disabled selected management features, including Easy Transferring, Expanded Item Interactions, Hotbar Changer, and Inventory Sorting.

• Disabled selected tools and gameplay features, including Pickarang, Pathfinder Maps, Torch Arrows, Automatic Recipe Unlock, and other unwanted mechanics.

• Disabled selected world-generation and mob features, including New Stone Types, No More Lava Pockets, Permafrost, and Crabs.

• Disabled selected client features, including Auto Walk, Camera Mode, Chest Searching, Greener Grass, Microcraft Helper, and other unwanted controls.

• Planned to disable all Quark Experimental settings.

These changes are intended to reduce feature overlap, avoid unwanted mechanics, and keep the modpack's gameplay consistent.

## 🧭 Travel & Community Play

• Waystones remain the intended travel system, with their crafting recipe corrected in the development notes.

• Continued work on server command permissions so travel options match the intended multiplayer experience.

• Retained multiplayer support through Simple Voice Chat, FTB Teams, FTB Chunks, and related tools.

• Continued configuration and testing to improve the shared experience for players on both single-player worlds and multiplayer servers.

## 💚 The Direction for Lunchtime

This overhaul is about making Lunchtime feel more complete: better performance tooling, more intentional recipes, structured quest progression, useful jobs and shops, richer exploration, and more cozy ways to build, cook, and play together.

**Lunchtime — a little more to explore, a little more to build, and a lot more to do together.**
