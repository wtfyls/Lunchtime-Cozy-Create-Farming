# Lunchtime — Performance, Progression & Exploration Overhaul

Lunchtime is getting a major overhaul focused on performance, cozy building, deeper exploration, better crafting progression, and more reasons to play together.

## ⚡ Performance & Stability Overhaul

- Added new performance-focused utilities, including Concurrent Chunk Management Engine, Async Logger, AsyncParticles, Fast Noise Mod, FastALI, Fast Suite, Fast Item Frames, and More Culling.
- Updated ModernFix and several libraries and supporting mods.
- Added SparseStructures and NoStructureOverlap to support more controlled structure generation.
- Continued config cleanup and performance testing, with particular attention to world generation and server stability.

## 📖 Quests & Progression

- Added FTB Quests and FTB Quests UI Overhaul.
- Added a custom welcome screen and quest background artwork.
- Expanded the tooling available for quest-based progression and rewards.
- Added Plushie Buddies, with plushies intended to be collectible quest rewards instead of ordinary craftable items.
- Added an extremely rare Star Wars reward to the reward-table setup.
- Introduced EMI and its supporting addons as the new recipe-viewing ecosystem, replacing the previous JEI-based setup.

## ⚒️ Crafting & Recipe Balancing

- Rebalanced Easy Villagers and addressed the easy-villager farm.
- Fixed the Waystone crafting recipe.
- Rebalanced aircraft recipes.
- Added more recipe-viewing integrations for Create and other modded crafting systems.

These changes aim to make progression more intentional while keeping farms, transportation, and automation useful without making everything too easy.

## 💼 Jobs & Player Economy

- Added Jobs+ and Jobs+ Tools.
- Added Shoppy, expanding the possibilities for player shops and an in-game economy.
- The standard Jobs+ professions include Alchemist, Builder, Digger, Enchanter, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, Lumberjack, Miner, and Smith.

The goal is to give players more things to work toward beyond exploration and collecting materials, while creating opportunities to specialize and trade with others.

## 🌍 World Generation & Exploration

- Added Integrated Dungeons and Structures, Integrated Villages, Moog's Mineshafts Reimagined, Moog's Temples Reimagined, and Moog's End Structures.
- Added Luki's Woodland Mansions and other structure-generation changes.
- Added Regions Unexplored and BetterDays.
- Reworked the structure lineup, removing several older YUNG's structure modules while retaining selected ones.
- Added SparseStructures and NoStructureOverlap for more controlled structure placement.
- Continued work on structure spacing and disabling selected vanilla structures.

Explore new terrain, caves, structures, and encounters while keeping the world generation focused on exploration rather than excessive structure density.

## 🍂 Seasons, Rivers & Underground Exploration

- Added Ecliptic Seasons, including integrations for Let's Do and Regions Unexplored.
- Added StreamsReflowing alongside the existing RiverRedux.
- Added Spelunkery for more cave and mining content.
- Added Icy's Better Horses for an improved horse experience.
- Added BetterDays to complement the new seasonal direction.

## 🏡 Cozy Building & Decoration

- Removed Let's Do Furniture from the listed mod set.
- Added Another Furniture, Cozy Home, Cozy Cafe, Dusty Decorations, and Building But Better.
- Retained established building and decoration options such as Chipped, Decorative Blocks, Supplementaries, and Immersive Paintings.
- Added Plushie Buddies for collectible decoration and quest rewards.

The aim is to give builders more options without losing Lunchtime's cozy, approachable style.

## 🍳 Cooking, Farming & Food

- Added Barbeque's Delight, Brewin' And Chewin', Butchery, Fishermens Trap, Simple Ice Cream, and Kaleidoscope Grilling.
- Removed Ender's Delight, Let's Do Brewery, Let's Do Camping, and Let's Do HerbalBrews from the supplied new mod list.
- Retained the core Farmer's Delight ecosystem, including Cultural Delights, Delightful, Alex's Delight, Chef's Delight, and the existing Kaleidoscope cooking mods.
- Continued balancing Easy Villagers and related farm progression.

The goal is to keep food, farming, and cooking central to the pack while making recipes and progression more consistent.

## 🐾 Mobs, Encounters & Additional Content

- Added Critters and Companions, Creeper Overhaul, Enderman Overhaul, Faunify, and Golem Overhaul.
- Added Cataclysm, Bosses'Rise, Combat Roll, DragonLoot, It Takes A Pillage, and other combat or encounter-related content.
- Added new supporting features for exploration and encounters.

Spawn limits and difficulty should be tested together to keep the world lively without overwhelming players or servers.

## 🎨 Interface, Audio & Multiplayer

- Added ImmersiveUI, Inventory Particles, Chat Heads, ChatAnimation, ExtraSounds Next, Minecraft Cursor, Emotecraft, and Online Emotes.
- Added Exposure Polaroid and EMF Compat: Core.
- Added e4all and additional FTB multiplayer tools.
- Kept Simple Voice Chat, Waystones, Xaero's Minimap, and Xaero's World Map in the listed setup.

## 🧭 Travel & Community Play

Waystones remain the intended travel system, with their crafting recipe corrected in the development notes. Server command permissions still need to be verified so the final setup matches the intended travel rules.

## 💚 The Direction for Lunchtime

This overhaul is about making Lunchtime feel more complete: better performance tooling, more intentional recipes, structured quest progression, useful jobs and shops, richer exploration, and more cozy ways to build, cook, and play together.

**Lunchtime — a little more to explore, a little more to build, and a lot more to do together.**
