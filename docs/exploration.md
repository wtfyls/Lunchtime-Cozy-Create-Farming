# Exploration and Collecting

Lunchtime adds content beyond a small starter base, so exploration can be about more than finding resources. Travel with an objective, bring enough supplies, and leave room in your inventory for the things you discover.

## Prepare before you leave

- Bring food, basic tools, lighting, and a way to store collected items.
- Note your route or use the map and navigation tools available in your release.
- Carry a spare tool or a backup plan for longer trips.
- If you are exploring with friends, agree on a meeting point and how you will return.

## Explore at your own pace

The mod list combines world-generation and exploration content with additional creatures, structures, and cave-focused adventures. Alex's Caves and Alex's Mobs are among the pack's adventure-oriented systems. What you encounter depends on your world, your installed version, and your progression.

Use the questbook to find exploration-related goals and check the item recipe viewer for new materials you bring home.

## World updates

World-generation changes are best experienced in newly generated terrain, and some changes may not appear in chunks that already exist. Before updating a world after a major biome or structure change, make a separate backup and read the release notes.

Do not delete your existing world to fix a normal gameplay problem. When in doubt, duplicate the save and test the update on the copy first.

## Bring discoveries home

Use what you find to improve your builds, cooking, automation, collections, and quest progression. Leave shared areas organized so other players can make use of resources too.
