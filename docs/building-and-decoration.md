# Building and Decoration

Lunchtime is meant to support many kinds of cozy builds: starter cottages, farms, bakeries, cafés, storage rooms, workshops, and shared community spaces.

## Make a base feel lived in

You do not need to decorate everything at once. Start with the useful spaces, then add details as you settle in:

- **Home:** beds, lighting, storage, and a safe place to return to.
- **Kitchen:** cooking stations with ingredients close by.
- **Garden:** crops, paths, composting or utility areas, and room to expand.
- **Workshop:** accessible Create machines with clear paths around moving parts.
- **Community space:** shared storage, a noticeboard, market stalls, or a café for multiplayer groups.

## Explore the building palette

The mod list includes a range of decoration, furniture, block-variation, and building tools. Chipped and Create: Copycats+ are two examples that can help you add variety to a build without changing its overall style.

Check the complete [mod list](mod-list-full.md) and recipe viewer to discover the blocks available in your release.

## A few practical habits

- Use consistent labels for shared storage.
- Leave space around machines and farms for future upgrades.
- Decorate paths and entrances so visitors can find their way around.
- Back up important worlds before installing pack updates or changing world-generation content.
