# Welcome to Lunchtime 🌿

Lunchtime is a cozy, multiplayer-friendly Minecraft modpack built around farming, cooking, Create automation, building, collecting, and exploring at your own pace.

This guidebook helps you discover the pack's main systems, find recipes, follow the questbook, troubleshoot problems, and get more out of playing with friends.

## What's in the pack?

- **Farming and food:** grow ingredients, explore cooking systems, and build a kitchen that suits your world.
- **Create and automation:** turn repetitive work into mechanical contraptions and production lines.
- **Building and decoration:** make cottages, cafés, farms, factories, and shared spaces feel like home.
- **Quests and progression:** use FTB Quests for guidance and goals across major pack systems.
- **Exploration and collecting:** travel beyond home, discover new content, and bring interesting finds back to your builds.
- **Multiplayer:** make a shared world more fun through teamwork, trading, and coordinated projects.

The precise content, quests, and balance can change between releases. The in-game questbook and the version you have installed are the source of truth for what is currently available.
