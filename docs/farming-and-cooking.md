# Farming and Cooking

Food is one of Lunchtime's main loops. Start with a small garden and a few recipes, then expand your kitchen as you discover what the pack offers.

## Start a useful farm

- Keep a few plots for crops you use frequently and expand when you discover new recipes.
- Store seeds and harvests separately from cooked meals so ingredients are easy to find.
- Grow more than you need for one meal if you intend to use ingredients for processing or automation.
- Make a little storage area near your cooking stations to reduce trips across your base.

## Discover recipes

The pack includes Farmer's Delight and food-related additions, including systems from the Let's Do series and Create integrations. The precise items and recipes depend on your installed release.

Use the recipe viewer to search for an ingredient or finished meal. Open its recipe to check the required ingredients and workstation. If an item has several possible recipes, make sure you are viewing the one available in Lunchtime.

## Build a kitchen that grows with you

You can start with simple cooking stations and later add more dedicated work areas, storage, and automated processing. Organizing ingredients near the stations that use them makes a larger kitchen easier to maintain.

Some food systems integrate with Create, allowing machines to assist with parts of food production. See [Create and Automation](create-and-automation.md) for ideas.

## Ingredient duplicates or missing recipes

Lunchtime's mod and recipe setup is curated to reduce unnecessary overlap, but recipe behavior can change as mods are updated.

If an item seems duplicated, missing, or impossible to craft:

1. Search the recipe viewer for both the ingredient and the finished item.
2. Check that you are using the recipe and workstation from the current pack version.
3. Check the pack changelog for recent recipe changes.
4. If it looks like a real bug, submit a [bug report](ISSUE_TRIAGE.md) with the item name, version, and a screenshot or log when useful.

## Cozy goals to try

- Grow the ingredients for a favorite meal.
- Build a pantry with labeled storage.
- Make a bakery corner or a café for your multiplayer group.
- Automate one repetitive food-processing step instead of automating everything at once.
- Share meals and ingredients with friends.
