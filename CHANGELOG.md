# Lunchtime — Quests, Shops, Economy & Skills Update v2

A major update focused on making Lunchtime more rewarding to play, with expanded quests, a player-driven economy, a new skills system, improved farming progression, Create Academy, and more ways to grow, craft, trade, explore, and collect.

This update also changes how the multiplayer economy works. Instead of relying on a large collection of predefined shops, players can build their own shops and trade with one another using Create: Numismatics.

## ✨ Added

### 📖 Quests & Progression

**•** Expanded the questbook with more things to do throughout the pack.

**•** Added more progression and quests for modded gameplay.

**•** Added questlines for Tom's Simple Storage, Spelunkery, Tetra, plushies, and other supported content.

**•** Expanded existing questlines and incorporated additional quest content.

**•** Improved the connection between quests, progression, rewards, and the economy.

**•** Added more meaningful rewards, including plushies and supporter-themed content.

**•** Added a rare Star Wars-themed reward.

### 🏗️ Create Academy

**•** Introduced **Create Academy**, a growing collection of visual guides for learning how to build Create farms and automated production systems.

**•** Added 3D visuals to help players understand farm layouts, machine placement, and how different components fit together.

**•** Added visual farm-building guides designed to make Create automation easier to understand, even for players who are new to the mod.

**•** Established Create Academy as an expandable collection, with new farms and guides planned to be added each week.

**•** Created a dedicated place to showcase farm designs and help players learn new automation techniques.

### 🪙 Create: Numismatics & Player Economy

**•** Added **Create: Numismatics**, introducing a Create-themed currency and trading system.

**•** Introduced coin-based trading and player-run Vendor shops.

**•** Added banking features for managing money through the Numismatics system.

**•** Shifted the economy toward player-created shops instead of relying on a large selection of predefined shop offers.

**•** Made player trading and shop building a bigger part of the multiplayer experience.

**•** Encouraged players to sell goods from their farms, fishing trips, crafting setups, and automated production lines.

### 🌱 Farming & Gardening

**•** Added **Gentle Seasons** to support Lunchtime's cozy farming and gardening experience.

**•** Added **Naturalist**, expanding the pack's wildlife and animal content.

**•** Continued expanding farming progression with useful crops, seeds, and resources.

**•** Improved the direction of farming quests to focus on things players can grow, harvest, craft, and trade.

### 🧵 New Content & Building

**•** Added Weavers Paradise.

**•** Added Create: Liquid Fuel to expand Create-based production possibilities.

**•** Added Create:LazyTick.

**•** Added Create Cobblestone for Create-based cobblestone generation.

**•** Added Create: Better Tom's Storage GUI for an improved storage interface.

**•** Added compatibility content for supported Let's Do mods, including Brewery and Farm&Charm.

**•** Added Every Compat integrations for Stone Zone and Wood Good.

**•** Added Soul fire'd.

### 🧰 Skills, Storage & Utility

**•** Added Pufferfish's Skills as the new skills system, replacing Jobs+ and Jobs+ Tools.

**•** Added Tom's Simple Storage and related quest content.

**•** Added Auto Stack Refill.

**•** Added Lunch Worlds.

**•** Added Snail Mail.

**•** Added Mining Utility: Reforged.

**•** Added Corpse and its Curios API compatibility mod as part of the revised death and item-recovery setup.

**•** Added Cobweb.

### 👋 New Player Welcome

**•** Simplified the welcome quest to make it easier for new players to understand the pack.

**•** Focused the introduction on what Lunchtime is about, useful keybinds, and how to get started.

**•** Reduced unnecessary introductory text so new players can get into the game faster.

## 🔧 Changed & Improved

### Questbook

**•** Transitioned away from FTB Quests and its UI overhaul as part of the questing changes.

**•** Reworked quest progression to better suit Lunchtime's cozy, vanilla-adjacent gameplay.

**•** Improved the focus on farming, cooking, building, automation, exploration, fishing, and ranching.

**•** Removed most shop-related entries from the questbook while keeping one shop-related quest to introduce players to trading.

**•** Shifted the economy away from predefined shop listings and toward player-created businesses.

**•** Removed outdated quest coin rewards where they no longer fit the updated economy.

**•** Expanded the integration of farm-building content and Create progression.

### Shops & Economy

**•** Removed Shoppy and moved toward a player-driven economy using Create: Numismatics.

**•** Reduced the number of predefined shop offers and removed most shop entries from the questbook.

**•** Kept one shop-related quest so players can discover the shop system without having the entire economy dictated by quests.

**•** Made creating and managing shops a player activity, allowing the community to establish its own marketplace.

### Skills & Progression

**•** Replaced Jobs+ and Jobs+ Tools with Pufferfish's Skills.

**•** Restored Tom's Simple Storage and integrated it into the quest progression.

**•** Continued improving the relationship between resource gathering, crafting, automation, and progression.

### Farming & Seasons

**•** Replaced Better Days with Gentle Seasons as part of the pack's evolving cozy farming experience.

**•** Continued balancing farming progression and crop-related gameplay.

**•** Improved the connection between growing resources and the player-driven economy.

### Create & Automation

**•** Replaced Create: Easy Stone Generators with Create Cobblestone.

**•** Expanded Create-related content with Create: Numismatics, Create: Liquid Fuel, and Create:LazyTick.

**•** Introduced Create Academy as a growing collection of visual farm-building guides.

### Death & Item Recovery

**•** Replaced the previous GraveStone Mod setup with Corpse.

**•** Removed the Better Gravestones texture pack alongside the previous gravestone setup.

**•** Adjusted the item-recovery setup to address the conflict with Dungeon Now Loading boss encounters.

### Technical & Performance Changes

**•** Replaced Radium Reforged with Radium Re-Reforged.

**•** Continued cleaning up the mod list by removing older utility, performance, compatibility, and interface mods that are no longer included.

**•** Updated the pack's utility and compatibility setup alongside the new content.
